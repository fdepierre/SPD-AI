# SPD-AI: Software Product Development with AI in Europe

This repository gathers working documents on how AI agents change the way software products are built, managed and governed, with a European focus: sovereignty of the tool chain and regulation (AI Act, NIS2, Cyber Resilience Act, GDPR).

The documents are written in French for now. A short summary of each is given below.

## Documents

| Document | Summary |
|---|---|
| [Le futur du Product Management à l'ère des agents IA](<Le futur du Product Management à l'ère des agents IA.md>) | What changes for Product Management when agents do a growing part of the building work: deciding what deserves to exist, passing on product knowledge, proving and accepting changes made by agents, organisation, economics and the European framework. It has a glossary and appendices covering the state of the art, existing thinking (including Marty Cagan and SVPG), points still to verify, and sources. |
| Développer avec l'IA : aujourd'hui et demain, avec un objectif de souveraineté européenne (to be added) | Developing software with AI tools today and tomorrow, while keeping control over models, hosting and data in Europe: data confidentiality levels, security rules for agents, infrastructure cost scenarios and European qualifications. |

## Status

These are version 1.0 working documents, not scientific papers. Each important claim is labelled as a documented fact, an actor's statement, a hypothesis, a recommendation or an open question. Some figures and claims have not yet been checked against their primary sources. They're marked as such, and the main document lists them in its appendix H.
